# Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We, the members of this project team (Delaney, Lise and Twinkle), and anyone who contributes to this repository, pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment include:

Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including in pull request reviews
Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them
Communicating clearly and in a timely way, especially when a deadline or task is at risk
Focusing on what is best for the project and the team as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

Insulting, demeaning or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
Public or private harassment of any kind
Dismissing or ignoring a contributor's work or ideas without explanation
Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in an academic or professional setting
Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including this GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, reviews and commit messages), our team chat, and in-person or online meetings. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to any member of the project team:

Delaney: <delaneypaigewoods@gmail.com>
Lise: <lisegong@gmail.com>
Twinkle: <jerushatwinklefrancis@gmail.com>

If the behaviour involves a member of the project team, or you are not comfortable reporting to the team, you may contact the DSCI 522 teaching team instead.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The project team will respect the privacy and security of the person who reports an incident.

Consequences

The project team is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. In deciding on a response, we will follow these guidelines:

Correction: For minor issues, such as unprofessional language, the person will receive a private message explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
Warning: For a more serious incident or repeated behaviour, the person will receive a warning with clear consequences if it continues.
Temporary restriction: For serious or sustained violations, the person may be temporarily asked not to interact with the project or its contributors.
Escalation: For violations within the team that cannot be resolved among ourselves, or for any form of harassment, the issue will be escalated to the DSCI 522 teaching team.
